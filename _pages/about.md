I am a PhD student at the [Bren School of Environmental Science & Management](https://www.bren.ucsb.edu), University of California, Santa Barbara, and a Graduate Student Associate at the [Broom Center for Demography](https://www.broomcenter.ucsb.edu).

My research sits at the intersection of environmental exposure and population health. I study **air pollution and reproductive health**, **climate-related environmental stressors**, and **population health inequality** — using geospatial analysis, satellite remote sensing, and applied statistics to ask who bears the burden of environmental hazards, and why.

Research
======
Current and recent projects include:

* **Early-life air pollution exposure and the timing of menarche in India** — with Prof. Kathy Baylis.
* **Coal mining hazards and social vulnerability in India** — recipient of the 2026 Jeanne X. Kasperson Student Paper Award (AAG Hazards, Risk, and Disaster Specialty Group).
* **Air pollution and social deprivation in India** — with Prof. Jayajit Chakraborty.
* **Spatial AI for wildfire risk: defensible space detection from satellite imagery** — with Prof. Sarah Anderson.

Background
======
Before starting my PhD in 2024, I completed an MS in Environment Management (Gold Medalist) at Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University and a BS in Botany at the University of Delhi. I have worked with the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi on disparities in air pollution exposure and disease burden in rural India, and with the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology on urban air quality in Delhi.

I serve as a peer reviewer for *Theoretical and Applied Climatology* and *Global Environmental Politics*.
